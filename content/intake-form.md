Title: **Free statistical trade audit — first ten**

Intro text:

> Send a raw broker export and you get back a statistical audit of your record:
> whether your results are distinguishable from chance, which of your "setups"
> survive correction for how many were examined, what your costs are actually
> doing to you, and — if you re-audit later — whether anything really changed.
> No signup, no newsletter, no journal to fill in. The report explains every
> term it uses in plain language.

## Fields

1. **Name or handle** (short text, required)
2. **Email** (email, required — the report goes here)
3. **Broker / platform** (dropdown, required):
   thinkorswim (Schwab) · NinjaTrader · Tradovate · TopstepX/ProjectX ·
   Rithmic (R|Trader) · Interactive Brokers · TradeStation · Other (say which)
4. **Account type** (dropdown, required):
   Personal account · Prop-firm evaluation · Prop-firm funded · Sim/paper
5. **Broker export file** (file upload, CSV, required)
   Helper text: *Export your full statement or trade history as CSV. Worried
   about what's in it? Use the redactor first — it strips your account number,
   balances, deposits and transfers in your own browser, nothing is uploaded:
   https://thomaswfreeman-lang.github.io/edgeaudit/web/redactor.html*
6. **Anything you already believe about your edge?** (long text, optional)
   Helper text: *e.g. "I'm best at NQ opens" or "my A+ setups carry me" — if
   you tell us before the audit, the report can test the claim directly.*

## Consent (two separate checkboxes — the second must NOT be pre-ticked)

**Required**

- [ ] I'm supplying this trade export so a statistical report can be produced
  for me. I understand the report describes this sample only, is not
  investment advice, is not a certification or attestation of my account, and
  does not predict future results.

**Optional**

- [ ] You may include my trades, in anonymised and aggregated form, in
  benchmark statistics (for example: what share of traders show an
  established edge, or how trades taken after a loss compare across
  accounts). No account numbers, no name, no individual trade is ever
  published or resold, and I can withdraw this by email at any time.

  Helper text under the checkbox: *Ticking this helps refine the audit and
  improve the whole system. Your report is identical whether or not you
  tick it.*

## Confirmation message (after submit)

> Got it. You'll have the report within 48 hours — usually much sooner. One thing now, one thing
> later: **now** — nothing in the report is advice, it's measurement.
> **Later** — in 30–60 days I'll offer to run your *new* trades through the
> same tests, free, so "did it get better?" gets an actual answer instead of
> a feeling. You can ignore that email if you want.

## Follow-up templates

**T+30–60 days (re-audit offer):**

> When you're ready, send your latest export (same place: [form link]). The
> re-audit compares the new stretch against the old one with an interval on
> the difference — improving, deteriorating, or indistinguishable. Free,
> same as before.

**T+60–90 days (outcome question — one line, log the answer):**

> Quick one — since the report: did you pass, reset, still trading, or stop?

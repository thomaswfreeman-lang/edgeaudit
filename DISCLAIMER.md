# What this is, and what it is not

EdgeAudit produces a **statistical report** describing a sample of trades that
was supplied to it. It is not an audit opinion, an attestation, a
certification, or a verification of any account.

- The output describes the sample provided. It is not a forecast, and it does
  not establish future performance.
- Nothing here is investment advice, financial advice, or a recommendation to
  take or avoid any trade, strategy, position size, or account.
- The report is only as accurate as the file supplied. Trades omitted from the
  export are omitted from the analysis.
- Forward simulations project a trader's own past distribution forward and
  assume they continue trading the same way. Real traders tilt, oversize and
  quit; none of that is modelled. Treat the figures as a floor on risk, not a
  ceiling.
- Prop evaluation rule presets are structural templates, not any firm's
  current terms. Confirm the numbers with the firm before relying on them.
- No accountant, auditor, or registered financial professional is involved in
  producing this report.

## Data handling

Trade data supplied for an audit is used to produce that audit. If you also
consent, an anonymised, aggregated form of it may be used to build benchmark
statistics — no account numbers, no names, no identifiable trades, and never
resold as raw data. Consent is separate, opt-in, and refusing it does not
change the audit you receive.

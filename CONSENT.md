# Audit intake — consent language

Use this verbatim on the intake form. Two separate checkboxes; the second must
not be pre-ticked and must not gate the first.

---

**Required**

☐ I'm supplying this trade export so a statistical report can be produced for
me. I understand the report describes this sample only, is not investment
advice, is not a certification or attestation of my account, and does not
predict future results.

**Optional**

☐ You may include my trades, in anonymised and aggregated form, in benchmark
statistics (for example: what share of traders show an established edge, or
how trades taken after a loss compare across accounts). No account numbers, no
name, no individual trade is ever published or resold, and I can withdraw this
by email at any time.

*Helper text under the checkbox:* Ticking this helps refine the audit and
improve the whole system. Your report is identical whether or not you tick it.

---

**Follow-up question, asked 60–90 days after delivery, one line, one reply:**

> Quick one — since the report: did you pass, reset, still trading, or stop?

That single answer is what eventually turns the archive into a model that can
predict outcomes. It's worth more than the trade data on its own, and it's
worthless if you start collecting it at audit 300 instead of audit 1.

Runs entirely on your machine

Strip your broker statement before you share it

A Schwab/thinkorswim statement contains far more than your trades: your account number, every running cash balance, deposits, withdrawals, bank transfers, dividends and interest. Nobody analysing your trading needs any of that.

This page has no server. There is no upload, no network request, no analytics, nothing stored. Your file is read by your own browser, stripped there, and handed back to you as a download. You can disconnect from the internet, and it will still work. View source — it's one file and about a hundred lines.

or drag a .csv here

What gets removed

  • Your account number
  • Every running balance column — this is what reveals account size
  • The cash-flow column in the equity ledger
  • Broker order reference numbers
  • Every non-trade row: deposits, withdrawals, bank transfers, dividends, interest, cash sweeps, daily balances
  • Every section that isn't trades: Order History, Positions, Account Summary

What's left

The fill lines only — side, quantity, instrument, price, fees. Enough to analyse how you trade, not enough to identify you or size your account.

Built for Schwab/thinkorswim combined account statements. Other broker exports pass through mostly unchanged, since they rarely contain balance or transfer data in the first place — check the preview before you send anything.
